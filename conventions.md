# Kata conventions

In `kiba_dachi`, `lead` is the foot on the side you are facing, not the arm used or the foot that just stepped. In Tekki Shodan, the stance stays on the east–west line: facing `N` means `both`, `W` means `left`, and `E` means `right`. Here, `facing` follows the head direction; `turn` describes the change from the previous facing.
